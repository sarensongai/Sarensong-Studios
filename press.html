Studio · paragraph

Sarensong Studios is an independent game studio founded in 2026. It is a team of one: design, code, art direction and story all come from a single developer, supported by an AI-assisted production pipeline built on Claude. The studio makes stylized, story-led games with handcrafted worlds that players want to come back to. Its debut project, Embertide, is in development.

Embertide · paragraph

When the sea swallows their mountain, a small clan of dwarves washes ashore on a strange island. Its cliffs hum, its tides keep time, and something deep beneath it is singing. Embertide is a story-led adventure about building a new hearth: exploring a hand-shaped island, keeping a stubborn clan together, and learning what the island's song is trying to say. Told like an old fable, it is short enough to finish and warm enough to remember.
