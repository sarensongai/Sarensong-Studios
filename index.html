A stylized island fable about dwarves.

Our debut game. A hand-shaped island, a stubborn clan of dwarves, and a story told the way old fables are told: simply, warmly, with something true underneath.

Pillar · World

An island you can read at a glance

Bold shapes, painterly colour and clean silhouettes. Every cove and cliff is placed by hand, so looking around is always worth it.

Pillar · Heart

Dwarves at the centre

Stone halls, forge-fire, old grudges and fierce loyalty. The clan is the soul of the game and the reason to keep playing.

Pillar · Story

Told like a fable

A focused, heartfelt tale you play through at your own pace. Short enough to finish, memorable enough to retell.

The studio

One developer. A full studio's toolkit.

Sarensong is a solo studio. Design, code, art direction and story all come from one desk, supported by a production pipeline built around AI tools.

Keeping the team tiny keeps the work personal. Every Sarensong game should feel made by someone who cares about every corner of it, because it was.

How we build

A one-person studio, built with Claude.

AI takes on the heavy lifting around the game, so the human hours go into the parts players actually feel.

  • In use

    Gameplay code and tools

    Claude Code pairs on gameplay systems, editor tooling and build automation, from the first prototype to the shipping build.

  • In use

    World and narrative

    Lore, quest outlines and dialogue drafts are developed with Claude, then rewritten by hand in the game's own voice.

  • In use

    Production

    Design docs, task planning, devlogs and store copy move faster, which keeps a solo schedule realistic.

  • Exploring

    Living characters

    R&D into in-game features powered by the Claude API, such as dwarves who react to what you do and a narrator who remembers your choices.
